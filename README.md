I care about software that feels emotionally clear on the surface and technically serious underneath.

Hopen is the clearest expression of that. It brings together product narrative, Flutter interface work, Go microservices, real-time transport, voice and video, authentication, authorization, observability, and platform architecture in one coherent system.

## Building now

- A social product built around smaller, time-bounded circles instead of endless feeds.
- A Flutter client shaped by clean architecture, strong interaction design, and real-time UX.
- A Go backend built around gRPC, events, messaging, and operational clarity.
- A platform where auth, policy, resilience, and infrastructure are part of the product quality bar.

## Selected work

- [Hopen](https://github.com/KevinHernot/hopen): a contextual social app built around Bubbles, small private groups designed for real friendship instead of public performance.
- [Backend architecture](https://github.com/KevinHernot/hopen/blob/main/docs/backend/architecture.md): Go services, gRPC boundaries, data systems, events, messaging, and operational design.
- [Flutter architecture](https://github.com/KevinHernot/hopen/blob/main/docs/flutter/architecture.md): client architecture, state management, interaction systems, and integration patterns.
- [Contextual culture](https://github.com/KevinHernot/hopen/blob/main/docs/gestion/marketing/Towards%20a%20contextual%20culture.md): the product thesis behind why this work exists at all.

## What I bring

- Founder-level ownership from product thesis to production detail.
- A mix of UI sensitivity and backend systems depth.
- Comfort working across Flutter, Go, real-time architecture, and security boundaries.
- A strong preference for coherence, readability, and long-horizon product quality.

## Current thesis

> The next generation of social software should feel more like a trusted room than a public stage.

I care about products that reduce noise, reward depth, and create the technical conditions for trust. That means smaller social surfaces, clearer incentives, stronger privacy boundaries, and systems that do not collapse into chaos as they scale.

## What I optimize for

- Product story and architecture reinforcing each other
- Interfaces that feel warm, precise, and intentional
- Distributed systems that stay understandable under pressure
- Security, resilience, and maintainability as first-class product features
- Long-horizon thinking over short-term spectacle

If you want the clearest signal on how I think and what I build, start with Hopen.
